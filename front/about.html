About us

Book Catalog is an online library where you can find and borrow books from every genre — fiction, non-fiction, biographies, sci-fi and more.

Browse the catalog, read reviews from other readers, share your own opinion and keep track of the books you love in your profile.

"A reader lives a thousand lives before he dies. The man who never reads lives only one." — George R.R. Martin

Privacy Policy

We store only what the library needs: your name, e-mail address, birth year, phone numbers, loans, reservations and reviews. Your password is never stored as text, only as a secure hash, so nobody can read it. We do not sell or give your data to third parties. Other readers see only your first name and the first letter of your last name next to your reviews.

Help & FAQ

How do I borrow a book?
Sign in, open a book and press "Borrow". The book appears in "My books" on your profile.
How long can I keep a book?
A loan lasts 14 days. After that the loan is marked as overdue.
What is a reservation?
When no copies are free, the button says "Reserve". You join the waiting list for the next free copy.
How do I leave a review?
Sign in, open the book page, choose a rating from 1 to 5, write a few words and press "Submit". One review per book.
